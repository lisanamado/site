About me:

This is the website I'm developing to publish my photos, ideas and projects. I'm using Photorama for Jekyll template, and GitHub hosting service. In a while you'll be able to see here data about me. Thanks for coming!

I'm a proffessional photographer with almost a decade of experience making pictures in Buenos Aires Province' ministry of education, as well as in diverse freelance assignments and in travels trough my loved Argentina. I'm also a diplomated history proffessor and a curious soul, always yearning for new experiencies and knowledges, ranging from ancestral indigenous identities and their relationship to land, to present developments and debates on AI, climate change and digital cultures. I love nature as much I valorate technologies, and would love it if my simple creations generate any thoughts and feelings on it.
